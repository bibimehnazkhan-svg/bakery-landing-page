Our Story

A small oven with a big heart

Golden Crumb started in 2015 as a single oven in a Gulberg kitchen. A decade later, we still bake the same way — slowly, in small batches, and never with shortcuts. Every loaf is shaped by hand before sunrise, so when you walk in, the racks are still warm.

  • Hand-shaped dough, fermented for up to 48 hours
  • Butter from local dairies, flour from Punjab mills
  • Custom cakes designed with you, not from a catalogue
